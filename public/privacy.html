DEXP Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026
Product: DEXP (package com.dexp.shop)
Operator: DEXP / Marlon Vargas · iattv7@gmail.com

DEXP is a dealer shop operations app. It is used by dealership staff (advisors, technicians, wash, valet, foreman, and similar roles) to run repair-order work, wash, loaners, appointments, and shop requests. It is not a consumer social app and is not directed at children.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the DEXP website and the DEXP Android app when a user signs in to a dealership that uses DEXP. Access is limited to people the dealership has authorized. Play Store install does not by itself grant access to shop data.

Information we collect

Depending on how the shop uses DEXP, we may collect:

  • Account information — email, display name, role, assigned modules, and the dealership the user belongs to.
  • Authentication data — sign-in credentials handled by Firebase Authentication (email and password). We do not receive or store card numbers for Play billing in the app.
  • Shop operations data — repair-order fields, vehicle identifiers (including VIN), customer name as entered by the shop, appointment rows, wash and loaner status, notes, and similar work records the dealer chooses to enter.
  • Photos and camera — images the user captures or uploads for VIN scan, appointment sheets, loaner condition, or similar shop tasks. These stay with that dealership’s DEXP data.
  • Device notifications — a push token so the app can send lock-screen alerts for shop requests (Android channel dexp_alerts).
  • Technical logs — basic app and hosting logs needed to keep the service running (for example, signed-in user id and error traces).

DEXP does not sell personal information. DEXP does not show third-party ads and does not use shop data for advertising.

How we use the information

  • To sign the user into the correct dealership and enforce role / module access.
  • To display and update live shop boards (tracker, operations, wash, loaners, appointments, tech, and related modules).
  • To send the user notifications they are assigned to receive.
  • To decode a VIN the user entered or scanned so the vehicle description can be filled.
  • To maintain, secure, and debug the service.

Where the data lives

DEXP uses Google Firebase (project dexp-5056c), including Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, and Firebase Hosting. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Dealership records are stored per dealer and are not a public directory.

Sharing

We share data only:

  • With staff of that same dealership who have been given DEXP access.
  • With infrastructure providers that host DEXP (Google Firebase / Google Cloud), under their terms.
  • If required by law.

We do not sell data to brokers or advertisers.

Retention and deletion

Shop records stay for as long as the dealership uses DEXP or until the dealership asks that an account or record be removed. A user may request deletion of their login by asking their dealer administrator or by emailing iattv7@gmail.com. We will delete or disable the account and associated login data. Operational repair-order history may be retained by the dealership as its business record.

Children

DEXP is intended for adults working in a dealership. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • You can change dealership on the login screen if you are invited to more than one roof.
  • You can disable notifications in Android system settings.
  • You can request account deletion as described above.
